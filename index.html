Appointments & rescheduling
  • Clients must be at least 16 years old.
  • No extra guests.
  • Reschedule at least 3 days in advance; notify Shana if cancelling.
  • No major style changes on appointment day.
  • Booking opens monthly on the 25th at 9 PM.
  • Before/after-hours appointments: an additional $95.
How to prepare your hair
  • Arrive with hair washed, blow-dried and product-free.
  • Optional: bring your preferred scalp oil.
  • Hair under 4 inches requires a $30 consultation.
Braiding hair & colours
  • Braiding hair is provided in colours 1, 1B and 2.
  • DM your desired colour at least 3 days before your appointment.
  • Request any other hair colours when booking.
Deposits & payment
  • Booking fees count toward your service total.
  • Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Zelle: Petergay Austin — 407-590-9478.
  • Cash App: $Shanna876.
  • Send a screenshot after payment.
